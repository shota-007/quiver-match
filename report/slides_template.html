Rent or buy it nearby

  • Real surf shops near the break, two per spot, with distance, address and a map link
  • Renting puts rental shops first; Buying puts board makers first
  • 52 of 54 spots covered (OpenStreetMap); Google Maps as a fallback
  • Idea from the owner of Jack's Surfboards, our first Tier 3 pilot partner

Safety from data, not guesses

  • Code rates each day from wave height, spot power and skill floor
  • Beginner at Black's: no-go all 3 days, so "Skip"
  • The Safety Agent finds Pacific Beach on days its own forecast rates "go"
  • The validator fixed the agent's board thickness live (yellow box)
  • {{SAFETY_PASSED}} attack and malfunction tests pass: prompt injection blocked, invented spots removed, no-go days forced to "Skip", loops and overspending stopped
